# Review, 2026-09-01 evening

A critical read of the whole project before the first upload window, written to
steer the unattended night that follows (`overnight/night.sh`) and the next working
session. The measurements quoted are from this repository's own logs and a fresh
benchmark run on the champion at commit fa10f1c.

## Where the engine stands

- Champion: python-chess move generation, (768 -> 512)x2 -> 32 -> 1 net trained on
  145M Lichess positions with fishnet labels, numba evaluation kernels, RFP, null
  move, killers, history, check extension, 20-ply Polyglot book, 3-4 man Syzygy.
- Fresh benchmark, one idle core: 107 knps, 9.35 us per node. Per node: legal move
  generation ~46% (28.6 us x ~0.15 calls), accumulator push/pop ~20%, board push/pop
  ~17%, NNUE evaluate ~14% (2.2 us). The docstring's claim that evaluation is the
  bottleneck is stale: after the numba kernels, move generation is.
- Ladder versus Stockfish skill 6/8/10 read 2660-2780 on the skill ladder's own
  scale. That is inflated: skill N is a depth-(N+1) Stockfish with deliberate random
  blunders, calibrated against real engines at 120 s + 1 s, and it was played here at
  20 s + 0.2 s. Comparable pure-Python engines in the literature: Sunfish ~1900-2000,
  Numbfish ~1800-2000, Antares (numba bitboards) ~2200. A defensible prior for this
  engine is 2200-2500 on a CCRL-like scale; the only calibrations that transfer are a
  Lichess bot account and the platform's own ladder from 4 September.

## Flaws found, ranked

1. **Time management burns the clock and is not measurable by the gauntlet.** The
   probe in `_clock.out` shows single moves of 12-14 s against a 3.3 s soft budget,
   47 s left by ply 30, 2.9 s left at the end, mean used/soft 3.06. Cause: the soft
   check runs only between iterations, the next iteration costs 3-4x, hard = 4x soft.
   Your own logs show flags on your own machine (32 in the 144-game slow run, 2 on
   the skill-8 rung, 3 in the run that promoted the 512 net). An SPRT at 8 s cannot
   see any of this, which is why 022-reserve and 023-timefix went nowhere.
2. **Nothing has been validated at 120 s + 0.5 s.** Every promotion was measured at
   8 s + 0.08 s, roughly depth 4-5 versus depth 6-7 at the real control.
3. **Process slipped on 1 Sep.** Experiments 022-025 are not journaled. 025's second
   run was 223 of 250 games both-failed, so its FAIL is an environment failure, and
   it bundled four changes.
4. **Search correctness:** quiescence stands pat in check on an evaluation never
   trained on checks; repetition history ignores positions after our own move; RFP
   is not guarded against mate-bound windows; history never decays; TT clears
   wholesale; a partially completed iteration is discarded.
5. **Training:** the 512 net was still improving at epoch 35 when the cosine schedule
   hit zero (val 0.005190 -> 0.005177 over the last five epochs). 145M of ~480M
   unique positions used. No king information, no output buckets. Book weighted by
   popularity across all ratings with an 8% floor.
